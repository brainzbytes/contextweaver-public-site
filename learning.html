Lesson 01 · Foundation

What are AI and large language models?

A language model predicts useful language from learned patterns. It can explain, summarize, and draft—but it does not know enterprise truth or hold authority to act.

AI
What it is

A probabilistic language engine that turns instructions and context into generated text, code, classifications, or structured output.

Why it matters
  • Makes complex knowledge easier to use
  • Accelerates analysis and drafting
  • Still requires grounding, validation, and control
Four core concepts
How it works
Key takeawayA model produces possibilities. A governed platform decides what is trusted, permitted, and recorded.

What is an AI Agent?

Foundation concept
A
60-second explanation

An Agent is a specialist role that uses a model to decide the next safe step, but only within typed instructions, approved knowledge, permitted tools, budgets, and stop conditions.

Why it matters

Turn repeatable expertise into a governed capability.

An Agent can apply a specialist role consistently while the platform preserves ownership, limits, approvals, and evidence. It augments a process; it does not become an unaccountable digital employee.

RAG, search, and memory

  • Citations and provenance

Identity, security, and governance
